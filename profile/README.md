# giftee Tech Vietnam

**Giftify everything, circulate the love through technology.** 🎁

giftee Tech Vietnam Co., Ltd is part of the giftee Group — the leading eGift company in Japan — founded to grow the eGift platform and build excellent products as giftee expands across Southeast Asia.

---

## About us

giftee Inc. was founded in Japan in 2010 and grew from personal gifts into enterprise eGift solutions, later expanding into Southeast Asia with branches in Vietnam, Malaysia, and Indonesia.

giftee Tech Vietnam was founded to grow that eGift platform — bringing together a development team focused on building excellent products to giftify everything and circulate the love through technology.

## Our vision & mission

- **Vision** — To provide services that nurture the ties between people, businesses, and townships through eGifts.
- **Mission** — To create a connected society built on better relationships by circulating love.

## What we build

giftee's core is the eGift platform business — an end-to-end service spanning eGift ticketing, distribution, and sales. Our work centers on:

- **giftee for Business** — eGift redemption at local stores through a proprietary eGift System, integrable with businesses' own websites so transactions process seamlessly.
- **eGift System** — a one-stop B2B solution for companies to send eGifts remotely and at scale, for campaigns or special occasions, across a range of communication platforms.
